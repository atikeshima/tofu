Make chord charts fast. Play from them right away.

An iPad app for chord charts, just chords and lyrics. There is no staff notation, so copying a song from your printed music is quick.

Free · No ads · iPad

Try changing the key with − and ＋.

The four bars transpose right here, a half step at a time. In the app, it is one tap too.

Key C

Tap a measure, then pick a root and a type.

  • Chords you have used in the song are listed by how often you used them. Repeat one with a single tap.
  • Choose jazz style (C-7, CΔ7) or standard style (Cm7, Cmaj7) for each song.
  • Import songs from MusicXML files exported by iReal Pro and other apps, and send your songs to iReal Pro.

Paste the lyrics at once, and print them too.

  • Paste all the lyrics and pour them into the measures. Keep verse 1 and verse 2 in the same measures.
  • Export as PDF in four ways: chords and lyrics, chords only, lyric sheet, or UTAHON Style with chords over the lyrics.

Bring your PDF scores together.

  • Import PDFs, or take photos with the camera or from your library. Reorder, crop, rotate and straighten pages.
  • Clean Up makes pencil charts easy to read. Write circles and marks with a pen while you play.

On stage, turn pages with a pedal.

  • Turn pages with a foot pedal, by tapping an edge, or by swiping. Tap D.S. or To Coda to jump to the right page.
  • The metronome shows the beat with sound, a swinging meter and a glowing screen edge. A tuner is built in.

Setlists keep the order and the keys.

  • Line up songs and PDF scores in the order you play them, and turn pages straight through. Change the key of each song just for that day.
