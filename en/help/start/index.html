Make a new song

  1. In the song list, tap "New Song" at the top right. In a narrow window it is just a plus sign (+).
  2. Type a name under "Title". It starts out as "Untitled", and whatever you type replaces it.
  3. Tap "Create". An empty chart opens with no measures.
  4. Tap "+4 Measures" in the toolbar above the chart to add measures.
  • While the song has no measures, a "+4 Measures" button also appears in the middle of the chart. from 1.4.0
  • If you leave the name empty, the song is called "Untitled".
  • If another song already has the same name, a number is added, like "My Song (2)".
  • When your song list is empty, "New Song" and "Add Sample Songs" appear in the middle of the screen. from 1.4.0

Find or sort songs

  1. In the song list, type part of a title in the "Search by title" box at the top.
  2. The list narrows down as you type.
  3. To change the order, tap the sort button next to the box. It looks like three lines that get shorter toward the bottom.
  4. Pick an order such as "Recently Opened", "Date Modified (Newest First)" or "Name (A to Z)".
  • Search ignores upper and lower case.
  • PDF scores are listed together with your songs.

Change the title, key, time signature or tempo

Songs you made in Tofu. For PDF scores, see "Rename or share a PDF score".

  1. On the chart screen, tap the song title at the very top. You can also tap the line of details under it (key, time signature, number of measures).
  2. "Song Settings" opens. You can also open it from "Song Settings" in the "…" menu.
  3. Change "Title", "Key", "Time Signature" or "Tempo". This is also where you enter "Lyrics by", "Music by", "Year" and "Memo".
  4. If the song has no tempo, tap "Add Tempo". It starts at 60. Use the minus (−) and plus (+) buttons to change it by 1.
  5. Tap "Done" when you're finished.
  • Changing "Key" does not move any chords. It only records what key the song is in.
  • To play the song in a different key, see "Change the key for the singer" (edit).
  • To remove the tempo, tap "Remove Tempo".

Duplicate or delete a song

  1. In the song list, tap the "…" button at the right end of the song's row.
  2. To make a copy, tap "Duplicate". The copy's name ends in "copy".
  3. To delete, tap "Delete".
  4. When "Delete this song?" appears, tap "Delete".
  • When the list is sorted by "Recently Opened", the copy appears right below the original.
  • Deleting can't be undone.
  • PDF scores have the same "…" button for duplicating and deleting.
  • If the song was in a setlist, the setlist keeps its row and shows "Missing song (title)".

Some songs couldn't be opened

When the song list shows a bar saying "1 song couldn’t be opened" (or a number of songs)

  1. Tap that bar.
  2. "Songs That Couldn’t Be Opened" opens. Each song shows why it couldn't be read.
  3. If you think you can repair a song by hand, tap "Copy Song Data" first to keep its contents.
  4. To get rid of a song, tap "Delete". When "Delete this song?" appears, tap "Delete" again.
  • Tofu never deletes an unreadable song on its own. You decide.
  • If the reason says the song was made with a newer version of Tofu, update Tofu in the App Store.
  • An unreadable PDF score shows up in the list as "Unreadable PDF Score". Tap it to see why, then choose "OK" or "Delete".
  • An unreadable setlist shows up as "Unreadable Setlist".

Get the how-to songs back

  1. In the song list, tap the gear button (Settings) at the top right.
  2. Under "Sample Songs", tap "Add Sample Songs".
  3. Two songs, "How to use Tofu" and "とうふの つかいかた", are added to your list.
  • Your existing songs stay as they are.
  • If a song with the same name exists, a number is added.
  • When your song list is empty, you can also use "Add Sample Songs" in the middle of the screen. from 1.4.0

See the tips again

  1. In the song list, tap the gear button (Settings) at the top right.
  2. Tap "Show Hints Again".
  3. You'll see "Hints will be shown again".
  • Tips you have dismissed will show up again on their screens.
  • The callouts on the performance screen and elsewhere come back too. from 1.4.0
  • The first-run walkthrough starts again the next time you launch the app. from 1.4.0
  • You can leave the walkthrough any time: tap "Skip" on the first card, or tap the dimmed area. from 1.4.0

Open this Help from the app from 1.4.0

  1. In the song list, tap the button with a question mark in a circle at the top right.
  2. You can also tap "Help" in Settings.
  3. On the chart screen, "Help" in the "…" menu opens the page about editing charts.
  4. When you're done reading, tap "Done" at the top left to go back to the app.
  • You need an internet connection to read these pages.
  • If the app is in Japanese, the Japanese pages open. Otherwise, the English pages open.
