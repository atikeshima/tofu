Make a new song

  1. In the song list, tap "New Song" at the top right. In Split View, when Tofu's window is narrow, it's just a add button.
  2. Type a name under "Title". It starts out as "Untitled", and whatever you type replaces it.
  3. Tap "Create". An empty chart opens with no measures.
  4. Tap "+4 Measures" in the row of buttons above the chart (the toolbar) to add measures.
  • While the song has no measures, a "+4 Measures" button also appears in the middle of the chart.
  • If you leave the name empty, the song is called "Untitled".
  • If another song already has the same name, a number is added, like "My Song (2)".
  • When your song list is empty, "New Song" and "Add Sample Songs" appear in the middle of the screen.

Find or sort songs

  1. In the song list, type part of a title in the "Search by title" box at the top. The box appears once your list has at least two items (songs and PDF scores combined).
  2. The list narrows down as you type.
  3. To change the order, tap the sort button (sort) next to the box.
  4. Pick an order such as "Recently Opened", "Date Modified (Newest First)" or "Name (A to Z)".
  • Search ignores upper and lower case.
  • For Japanese titles, search also treats hiragana and katakana, and full-width and half-width characters, as the same.
  • Imported PDF sheet music (PDF scores) is listed together with your songs.

Change the title, key, time signature or tempo

Songs you made in Tofu. For PDF scores, see "Rename or share a PDF score".

  1. On the chart screen, tap the song title at the very top. You can also tap the line under it (key, time signature, number of measures).
  2. "Song Settings" opens. You can also tap more_horiz at the top right of the chart screen, then "Song Settings".
  3. Change "Title", "Key", "Time Signature" or "Tempo". This is also where you enter "Lyrics by", "Music by", "Year" and "Memo".
  4. If the song has no tempo, tap "Add Tempo". It starts at 60 BPM. Use "−" and "+" to change it one BPM at a time.
  5. Tap "Done" when you're finished.
  • "Key" is the key the chart is written in (the original key). Changing it doesn't transpose any chords.
  • To play the song in a different key, see "Change the key for the singer".
  • To remove the tempo, tap "Remove Tempo".

Duplicate or delete a song

  1. In the song list, tap more_horiz at the right end of the song's row.
  2. To make a copy, tap "Duplicate". The copy's name ends in "copy".
  3. To delete, tap "Delete".
  4. When "Delete this song?" appears, tap "Delete".
  • When the list is sorted by "Recently Opened", the copy appears right below the original. If you've never opened the original, the copy appears after the songs you have opened.
  • Deleting can't be undone.
  • PDF scores have the same more_horiz button for duplicating and deleting.
  • If the song was in a setlist, its spot stays in the setlist, shown as "Missing song (title)".

Some songs couldn't be opened

When the song list shows a notice saying "1 song couldn’t be opened" (or a number of songs)

  1. Tap that notice.
  2. "Songs That Couldn’t Be Opened" opens.
  3. Each song shows its ID (a string of letters and numbers) and the reason it couldn't be read. The title isn't shown, and the reason is a short technical code.
  4. If you'd like someone to try fixing it, tap "Copy Song Data" first. The song's contents are copied as text; paste them into a note or an email and send it to them.
  5. To get rid of a song, tap "Delete". When "Delete this song?" appears, tap "Delete" again.
  • Tofu never deletes an unreadable song on its own. You decide.
  • If the reason starts with "newerMajorVersion", the song was made with a newer version of Tofu. Update Tofu in the App Store.
  • An unreadable PDF score shows up in the list as "Unreadable PDF Score". Tap it to see why, then choose "OK" or "Delete".
  • An unreadable setlist shows up as "Unreadable Setlist".

Get the sample songs (how-to songs) back

  1. In the song list, tap the Settings button (settings) at the top right.
  2. Under "Sample Songs", tap "Add Sample Songs".
  3. Two songs are added: "How to use Tofu" and its Japanese version, "とうふの つかいかた".
  • Your existing songs stay as they are.
  • If a song with the same name exists, a number like "(2)" is added.
  • When your song list is empty, you can also use "Add Sample Songs" in the middle of the screen.

See the tips again

  1. In the song list, tap the Settings button (settings) at the top right.
  2. Tap "Show Hints Again".
  3. You'll see "Hints will be shown again".
  • Hints you have dismissed come back on their screens, including the speech-bubble hints on the performance screen.
  • The welcome tour you saw the first time you opened the app starts again the next time you launch it.
  • You can leave the welcome tour any time: tap "Skip" on its first screen, or tap the dimmed area around it.

Open this Help from the app

  1. In the song list, tap help_outline at the top right.
  2. You can also tap "Help" in Settings.
  3. On the chart screen, "Help" under more_horiz at the top right opens the page about editing charts.
  4. When you're done reading, tap "Done" at the top left to go back to the app.
  • You need an internet connection to read these pages.
  • If the app is shown in Japanese, the Japanese pages open. Otherwise, the English pages open.
