Copy and paste measures

  1. Touch and hold the measure you want to copy. You can also tap "Select" in the toolbar and then tap a measure.
  2. To select several, keep the selection and slide your finger sideways across the measures.
  3. Tap "Copy". You'll see "Copied 4 measures" or similar.
  4. Tap the measure just before where you want to paste. That measure becomes the selection.
  5. Tap "Paste After". The copied measures go in after the selected one.
  6. Tap "Stop Selecting" when you're done.
  • "Paste After" inserts. Nothing is replaced; the measures after it move along.
  • To replace the selected measures instead, tap "Overwrite Selection" and then "Overwrite".
  • To select measures that are off screen, scroll first, then touch and hold the last measure.
  • Handwriting is not copied.
  • When you start selecting, a short tip explains how to select a range.

Delete or cut measures

  1. Touch and hold a measure to select it. Slide sideways to select a range.
  2. To delete, tap "Delete". When "Delete the selected measures?" appears, tap "Delete".
  3. To move measures, tap "Cut". They disappear and are ready to paste.
  4. Touch and hold the measure just before where they should go, then tap "Paste After".
  • You can't delete every measure in a song. At least one has to stay.
  • "Undo" brings deleted measures back.
  • To delete just one chord, use "Delete Chord" in the chord entry panel.

Undo a mistake

  1. On the chart screen, tap "Undo" at the top right. It's the button with an arrow curving to the left.
  2. If you went back too far, tap "Redo" next to it, the arrow curving to the right.
  • You can go back up to 20 steps.
  • The chord entry panel has the same two buttons at the top.
  • The undo history is cleared when you open another song or quit the app.

Write notes on the chart by hand

  1. Above the chart, switch from "Text" to "Draw".
  2. The drawing toolbar shows three pens. Each one is shown as a sample of the line it draws.
  3. Tap a pen and write on the chart.
  4. To erase, tap "Eraser" and rub over what you want to remove.
  5. Tap the selected pen again to change its "Color", "Width" and "Opacity".
  6. Tap "Text" when you're finished.
  • While drawing, scroll the chart with two fingers. One finger draws.
  • To clear all handwriting, tap "Delete All Handwriting". "Undo" brings it back.
  • Handwriting stays put even if the chart is laid out differently later. If it ends up in the wrong place, erase it and write it again.
  • Your pen settings are the same in every song.

Change the key for the singer

  1. In the toolbar above the chart, use the buttons on either side of "Transpose".
  2. The minus (−) button moves down a half step, and the plus (+) button moves up a half step.
  3. To go back to the original key, tap "Back to Original Key".
  • The song is still stored in its original key. Only what you see changes.
  • After transposing, sharps and flats follow the new key.
  • Chords you enter while transposed are saved correctly in the original key.
  • A setlist can change the key just for one gig.

The toolbar disappeared

  1. Scroll the chart all the way back to the top. The toolbar (the row with "+4 Measures", "Edit All at Once" and so on) comes back.
  2. To keep it showing all the time, tap the "…" button on the chart screen.
  3. Check "Always Show Toolbar".
  • The toolbar hides when you scroll further into the chart, so you can see more of it.
  • The buttons for a selection and the drawing toolbar don't hide.
  • When the toolbar hides, a note at the top of the chart explains how to get it back. Tap "Got It" and it won't show again.
