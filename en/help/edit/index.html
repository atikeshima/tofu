Copy and paste measures

  1. Touch and hold the measure you want to copy. You can also tap "Select" in the toolbar and then tap a measure.
  2. To select several, start from the selected measure and slide your finger sideways across the measures.
  3. Tap "Copy". You'll see "Copied 4 measures" or similar.
  4. Tap the measure just before where you want to paste. The earlier selection is cleared and only that measure is selected. The copied measures are still remembered.
  5. Tap "Paste After". The copied measures go in after the selected one.
  6. Tap "Stop Selecting" when you're done.
  • "Paste After" inserts. Nothing is replaced; the following measures shift over to make room.
  • To replace the selected measures instead, tap "Overwrite Selection". The confirmation shows how many measures will be replaced; tap "Overwrite".
  • To extend the selection off screen, scroll, then touch and hold the last measure you want. Everything in between is selected.
  • Handwriting is not copied.
  • When you start selecting, a short tip explains how to select a range.

Delete or cut measures

  1. Touch and hold a measure to select it. Slide sideways to select a range.
  2. To delete, tap "Delete". When "Delete the selected measures?" appears, tap "Delete".
  3. To move measures, tap "Cut". They disappear and are ready to paste.
  4. Touch and hold the measure just before where they should go, then tap "Paste After".
  • You can't delete every measure in a song. At least one has to stay.
  • undo ("Undo") brings deleted measures back.
  • To delete just one chord, use "Delete Chord" in the chord entry panel.

Undo a mistake

  1. On the chart screen, tap undo ("Undo") at the top right.
  2. If you went back too far, tap redo ("Redo") next to it.
  • You can go back up to 20 steps.
  • The chord entry panel has the same two buttons at the top.
  • The undo history is cleared when you open another song or quit the app.

Write notes on the chart by hand

  1. Above the chart, switch the mode from "Text" (normal editing) to "Draw".
  2. The drawing toolbar shows three pen buttons. Each button shows the color and width of the line that pen draws.
  3. Tap a pen and write on the chart.
  4. To erase, tap "Eraser" and drag over what you want to erase.
  5. Tap the selected pen again to change its "Color", "Width" and "Opacity".
  6. Tap "Text" when you're finished. This takes you back to normal editing.
  • While drawing, scroll the chart with two fingers. One finger draws.
  • To clear all handwriting, tap "Delete All Handwriting" in the drawing toolbar. undo ("Undo") brings it back.
  • Handwriting doesn't move with the measures. It keeps its position from the left edge of the row it was written on, so if measure widths or the measures in a row change, it may no longer line up. If it ends up in the wrong place, erase it and write it again.
  • Pen settings are shared across all songs.

Change the key for the singer

  1. In the toolbar above the chart, use the remove and add buttons on either side of "Transpose".
  2. remove moves down a half step, and add moves up a half step.
  3. To go back to the original key, tap "Back to Original Key".
  • Tofu keeps the chords you entered. Transposing only changes how they're shown on screen and in PDFs.
  • After transposing, sharps and flats follow the new key.
  • You can enter chords in the transposed key. Tofu converts them, so they're correct when you go back to the original key.
  • A setlist can change the key just for one gig.

The toolbar disappeared

  1. Scroll the chart all the way back to the top. The toolbar (the row with "+4 Measures", "Edit All at Once" and so on) comes back.
  2. To keep it showing all the time, tap more_horiz at the top right of the chart screen.
  3. Turn on "Always Show Toolbar".
  • The toolbar hides when you scroll down, to give the chart more room.
  • The row of buttons you get while measures are selected ("Copy", "Delete" and so on) and the drawing toolbar don't hide.
  • When the toolbar hides, a note at the top of the chart explains how to get it back. Tap "Got It" and it won't show again.
