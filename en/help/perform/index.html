Turn pages on the performance screen

  1. On the chart screen, tap "Perform" at the top right. In Split View, when Tofu's window is narrow, it's a play_arrow button.
  2. Tap within about 1 inch (3 cm) of the right edge of the screen for the next page, or near the left edge for the previous page.
  3. You can also swipe sideways.
  4. A page-turn pedal works too.
  • Keys that go to the next page: Down Arrow, Right Arrow, Page Down.
  • Keys that go back: Up Arrow, Left Arrow, Page Up.
  • A pedal works if it sends one of these keys. Pedals that send Space or Enter won't turn pages. Some pedals let you change which key they send, so check the pedal's manual.
  • The screen won't go to sleep while the performance screen is open.
  • The first time you open it, a callout shows how to turn pages.

Show the performance menu or change what's shown

  1. Tap the middle of the screen (not the strips about 1 inch / 3 cm wide at the left and right edges, which turn pages). The performance menu appears at the top. The first time, a green frame shows where to tap. The small see-through circle at the top left does the same.
  2. Tap the Display Options button (tune) and choose "Chords + Lyrics", "Chords Only", "Lyric Sheet" or "UTAHON Style (Chords Over Lyrics)".
  3. In the same Display Options, turn "Include Title and Credits" and "Include Memo" on or off.
  4. In a dark room, tap "Dark Display" in Display Options. The background turns dark and the text turns light.
  5. When two pages are side by side in landscape, choose how they turn under "Page Turn" in the performance menu. "Two Pages at a Time" replaces both pages with the next two. "One Page at a Time" moves on one page at a time.
  6. To hide the menu, tap keyboard_arrow_up at its left end.
  7. To leave the performance screen, tap "Close" at the right end of the menu.
  • The small circle never closes the screen. Only "Close" does.
  • For PDF scores, Display Options has only "Dark Display".
  • Your choices are kept for next time. "Dark Display" is shared by songs and PDF scores.
  • In Dark Display, sign colors and highlighter marks change to colors you can see on the dark background. Switch back to the normal display and they're back to their own colors.
  • In Split View, when Tofu's window is narrow, buttons that don't fit are grouped under more_horiz in the menu. The metronome and tuner stay in the menu as icons. Touch and hold an icon to see its name.

Jump to the right page from D.S. or To Coda

  1. On the performance screen, tap D.S. or To Coda on the chart.
  2. Tofu jumps to the page you need, and the sign you're jumping to lights up for about two seconds.
  • D.S. goes to the segno, To Coda goes to the coda, and D.C. goes to page 1.
  • Tapping an end-repeat sign (:‖) takes you back to the page where the repeat starts.
  • Tapping a 1st ending takes you to the page with the 2nd ending.
  • This works for songs made in Tofu, not for PDF scores.
  • In a setlist, jumps stay within the same song.

Zoom in on the chart

  1. Tap "Zoom" in the performance menu.
  2. Drag the slider to anywhere from 100% to 200%.
  3. To go back, tap "Reset to 100%".
  4. For songs made in Tofu, "Fit to Margins" removes the top and bottom margins of the page so the chart fills the screen.
  • The zoom is saved for each song and PDF score, so it's the same next time.
  • Pinching out zooms in temporarily. Turning the page returns to your "Zoom" setting.
  • While zoomed with two fingers, drag with one finger to look around.
  • When zoomed, the chart can extend past the page edge into the empty space around it instead of being cut off.

Use the metronome

  1. Tap "Metronome" in the performance menu. The metronome buttons appear below the menu.
  2. Tap play_arrow ("Start"). It turns into stop; tap that to stop.
  3. Change the tempo one BPM at a time with remove and add. Hold one down to keep changing it.
  4. Tap the tempo number to enter a new one, then tap "Set".
  5. You can also use tap tempo: in the screen that opens when you tap the tempo number, tap "Tap" along with the beat, then tap "Set".
  6. To watch the beat without sound, tap volume_up ("Mute").
  • The beat also shows on a meter that swings like a pendulum, and as a glow around the edge of the screen.
  • Starting the metronome or changing the tempo saves that tempo in the song, so it's the same next time.
  • For PDF scores, you can also pick the "Time Signature".
  • When you hide the performance menu, a small metronome window ("Mini") stays on top of the chart.
  • Drag the small window anywhere. Drag it to the left or right edge to switch it to a tall, vertical layout.
  • Choose its look with "Mini" in the metronome: "Buttons" or "Meter". "Buttons" shows a blinking light, remove, the tempo, add and play_arrow. "Meter" has a swinging meter instead of the blinking light.
  • The small window shows the current tempo between its remove and add buttons. Tap the number to enter a new one. from 1.6.0
  • chevron_left ("Fold") shrinks the window to a blinking light and the tempo.
  • While the metronome is stopped, the "Fold" button turns into close ("Close"). Tap it to close the small window.
  • The metronome stops when you close the performance screen, and when a setlist moves to another song.

Tune with the tuner

  1. Tap "Tuner" in the performance menu.
  2. The first time, allow Tofu to use the microphone.
  3. Play a note. The note name and a needle appear. You're in tune when the needle is centered and turns green.
  4. To change the reference pitch (such as A = 440 Hz), use remove ("Lower Reference Pitch") and add ("Raise Reference Pitch") on either side of the number.
  5. To tune by ear, choose a note under "Reference Tone".
  6. Tap volume_up ("Play Reference Tone"). Tap again to stop.
  • Opening the tuner stops the metronome.
  • If you see "Tofu can’t use the microphone. …", tap "Open Settings" next to it and allow microphone access for Tofu.
  • Quiet instruments, such as an electric bass not plugged into an amp, may not be picked up.

Write on a PDF score

PDF scores. For songs made in Tofu, see "Write notes on the chart by hand".

  1. Tap the PDF score in the list. It opens on the performance screen.
  2. Tap the middle of the screen to show the performance menu (not the strips about 1 inch / 3 cm wide at the left and right edges, which turn pages), then tap "Write".
  3. Pick a pen and write. ("Write" works like "Draw" for songs.) To erase, tap "Eraser" and drag over the writing.
  4. Tap "Done" when you're finished.
  • While writing, tapping the screen doesn't turn pages. A pedal still does.
  • The pen types, colors and widths are shared with handwriting in songs.
  • You can undo and redo your writing.
  • You can't write during a setlist. Open the PDF score on its own to write on it.
