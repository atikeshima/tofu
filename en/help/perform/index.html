Turn pages on the performance screen

  1. On the chart screen, tap "Perform" at the top right. In a narrow window it's a play triangle.
  2. Tap near the right edge (about 3 cm) for the next page, or near the left edge for the previous page.
  3. You can also swipe sideways.
  4. A page-turn pedal works too.
  • Pedals that send the ↓, →, or Page Down key go forward. ↑, ←, or Page Up go back.
  • Pedals that send Space or Enter aren't supported.
  • The screen won't go to sleep while the performance screen is open.
  • The first time you open it, a callout shows how to turn pages. from 1.4.0

Show the performance menu or change what's shown

  1. Tap anywhere inside the left and right edges (about 3 cm each) to show the performance menu at the top. The first time, a green frame shows where to tap. The small see-through circle at the top left does the same. from 1.4.0
  2. Under "Display Options", choose "Chords + Lyrics", "Chords Only", "Lyric Sheet" or "UTAHON Style (Chords Over Lyrics)".
  3. In the same place, turn "Include Title and Credits" and "Include Memo" on or off.
  4. For two-page spreads in landscape, choose "Two Pages at a Time" or "One Page at a Time" under "Page Turn".
  5. To hide the menu, tap the upward arrow at its left end.
  6. To leave the performance screen, tap "Close" at the right end of the menu.
  • The small circle never closes the screen. Only "Close" does.
  • "Display Options" is only for songs made in Tofu. PDF scores don't have it.
  • Your choices are kept for next time.
  • In a narrow window, buttons that don't fit are grouped under "…" in the menu.

Jump to the right page from D.S. or To Coda

  1. On the performance screen, tap D.S. or to Coda on the chart.
  2. Tofu jumps to the page you need, and the sign you're jumping to lights up for about two seconds.
  • D.S. goes to the segno, to Coda goes to the coda, and D.C. goes to page 1.
  • Tapping an end-repeat sign (:‖) takes you back to the page where the repeat starts.
  • Tapping a 1st ending takes you to the page with the 2nd ending.
  • This works for songs made in Tofu, not for PDF scores.
  • In a setlist, jumps stay within the same song.

Zoom in on the chart

  1. Tap "Zoom" in the performance menu.
  2. Drag the slider to anywhere from 100% to 200%.
  3. To go back, tap "Reset to 100%".
  • The zoom is saved for each song and PDF score, so it's the same next time.
  • Spreading two fingers zooms in only for the moment. Turning the page goes back to the "Zoom" setting.
  • While zoomed with two fingers, drag with one finger to look around.

Use the metronome

  1. Tap "Metronome" in the performance menu. A metronome row appears below the menu.
  2. Tap the play triangle ("Start"). It turns into a square; tap that to stop.
  3. Change the tempo by 1 with the minus (−) and plus (+) buttons. Hold one down to keep changing it. from 1.4.0
  4. Tap the tempo number to type a new one, then tap "Set".
  5. You can also set the tempo by tapping "Tap" in time with the beat.
  6. To watch the beat without sound, tap the speaker button ("Mute").
  • The beat also shows on a swinging meter and as a glow around the edge of the screen.
  • Starting the metronome or changing the tempo writes that tempo into the song.
  • For PDF scores, you can also pick the "Time Signature".
  • When you hide the performance menu, a small metronome window ("Mini") stays on top of the chart. from 1.4.0
  • Drag the small window anywhere. Push it against the left or right edge and it stands up vertically. from 1.4.0
  • Choose its look with "Mini" in the metronome row: "Buttons" or "Meter". from 1.4.0
  • The small arrow button ("Fold") shrinks the window to a blinking dot. from 1.4.0
  • The metronome stops when you close the performance screen, and when a setlist moves to another song.

Tune with the tuner

  1. Tap "Tuner" in the performance menu.
  2. The first time, allow Tofu to use the microphone.
  3. Play a note. The note name and a needle appear. You're in tune when the needle is centered and turns green.
  4. To change the reference pitch (such as 440 Hz), use the buttons on either side: minus is "Lower Reference Pitch", plus is "Raise Reference Pitch".
  5. To tune by ear, choose a note with the note button ("Reference Tone").
  6. Tap the speaker button ("Play Reference Tone"). Tap again to stop.
  • Opening the tuner stops the metronome.
  • If you see "Tofu can’t use the microphone.", allow microphone access for Tofu in the iPad's Settings app.
  • Quiet instruments, such as an electric bass not plugged into an amp, may not be picked up.

Write on a PDF score

PDF scores. For songs made in Tofu, see "Write notes on the chart by hand".

  1. Tap the PDF score in the list. It opens on the performance screen.
  2. Tap anywhere inside the left and right edges (about 3 cm each) to show the performance menu, then tap "Write".
  3. Pick a pen and write. To erase, tap "Eraser" and rub over the writing.
  4. Tap "Done" when you're finished.
  • While writing, tapping the screen doesn't turn pages. A pedal still does.
  • The pens are the same ones you use for handwriting in songs.
  • You can undo and redo your writing.
  • You can't write during a setlist. Open the PDF score on its own to write on it.
