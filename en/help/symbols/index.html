Add measures

  1. To add at the end, tap "+1 Measure" or "+4 Measures" in the toolbar above the chart.
  2. To add in the middle or at the start, touch and hold the measure next to where you want the new ones. You can also tap "Select" in the toolbar and then tap a measure.
  3. Tap "Add Empty Measures Before" or "Add Empty Measures After".
  4. Tap "Stop Selecting" when you're done.
  • You get as many empty measures as you have selected. Select four, and four are added.
  • The measures stay selected afterward, so you can tap again right away.
  • In the chord entry panel, "Add a measure and go to it" on the last measure also adds one.
  • On the "Edit All at Once" screen, "Add Here" between measures adds one too.

Add repeat signs (‖: :‖) and 1st and 2nd endings

  1. Tap the first measure of the repeat and open the "Signs" tab.
  2. Under "Bar Lines", tap "‖:" in the "Left" column.
  3. Open the last measure of the repeat and tap ":‖" in the "Right" column.
  4. For a 1st ending, use "Endings (1st, 2nd)": tap "Start of Ending 1" on its first measure and "End of Ending 1" on its last.
  5. For an ending that is only one measure long, tap "Ending 1 Only" or "Ending 2 Only".
  • Anything you tap on the "Signs" tab applies to that measure right away. Tap it again to remove it.
  • Signs are set one measure at a time. You can't apply them to a selected range.

Add segno, coda, D.S. or the simile mark (%)

  1. Tap the measure and open the "Signs" tab.
  2. In the "Signs" section, tap "Segno" or "Coda".
  3. Text signs such as to Coda and D.S. al Coda are buttons in the same section.
  4. For a measure that repeats the one before, go to "Repeat the measure before" and tap "%" ("Same as the measure before") or "%%" ("Same as the two measures before").
  • Coda (where you jump to) and to Coda (where you jump from) are different signs.
  • A measure marked with % keeps its chords hidden, not deleted. Remove the % and they come back.
  • On the performance screen, tapping D.S. or to Coda jumps to the page you need.

Name sections or change their order

  1. Tap the first measure of the section and open the "Signs" tab.
  2. Under "Section Name", tap a name such as A or Verse.
  3. For any other name, tap "Custom…", type the name and tap "Set".
  4. To change the order of sections, tap "Reorder Sections" in the "…" menu on the chart screen.
  5. Touch and hold the "≡" handle of a section and drag it where you want it. The up and down arrow buttons also move it.
  6. Tap "Done".
  • To remove a section name, tap the selected name again.
  • One "Undo" puts the whole reorder back.

Make the rows match the printed music

  1. Tap the measure that should start a new row and open the "Signs" tab.
  2. Under "Row Break", tap "Start New Row Here".
  3. To undo it, tap "Continue".
  4. For the same number of measures in every row, open "Song Settings" and choose "4 measures" or "8 measures" under "Measures per Row".
  • "Start New Row Here" prints nothing on the page. Only the screen shows a small mark on that measure.
  • With "Fit to Content", the number of measures per row depends on how much is in them.

Change the time signature partway through

  1. Tap the measure where the time signature changes and open the "Signs" tab.
  2. Under "Time Signature of This Measure", tap the time signature.
  3. To go back to the song's time signature, tap "Follow Song".
  • A time signature is shown only where it differs from the measure before.
  • To change the time signature for the whole song, use "Time Signature" in "Song Settings".
