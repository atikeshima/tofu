Entering lyrics

Type lyrics one measure at a time

  1. Tap the measure and open the "Lyrics" tab.
  2. Type only the words sung in this measure. If one printed line spans several measures, split it up measure by measure.
  3. Press Return to go on to the next measure's lyrics.
  4. To add a second verse, tap "Add Verse After". Tap a verse's line to choose which verse you're typing.
  5. Tap "Done" when you're finished.
  • Spaces between words show up as space on the chart. You can use extra spaces to nudge words into place.
  • A hyphen, as in An-oth-er, is printed just as you type it.
  • To remove a verse, tap "Delete This Verse". To add a verse in front, tap "Add Verse Before".
  • What you typed is also saved when you move with ‹ › or tap "Done".

Paste lyrics and place them into measures

  1. Tap "Pour Lyrics" in the toolbar above the chart. The "Add Lyrics in Bulk" screen opens.
  2. Paste the lyrics. To read them from a photo of a lyric sheet, tap "Read from Photos", or "Read from Camera" to take one now.
  3. Fix any misread words, then tap "Use These Lyrics". A lyric strip appears below the chart.
  4. In the strip, drag your finger over the words for one measure.
  5. Tap the measure on the chart where they belong. The words go into that measure and disappear from the strip.
  6. Keep going the same way: drag, then tap a measure.
  • For English lyrics, you can also tap a single word to select it.
  • To place the second verse, tap the verse button in the strip ("Verse 2" or "+ Verse 2") first.
  • "Close Lyric Strip" closes the strip. Lyrics you haven't placed are kept.
  • Pick up where you left off with "Continue Pouring" in the toolbar.
  • To throw away lyrics you don't need, use "Discard Unpoured Lyrics".
  • Photos are read on this iPad. They are never sent anywhere.
  • The first time you pour lyrics, callouts point to where to drag and where to tap. from 1.4.0

Fix lyrics that are off by a measure

  1. Tap "Edit All at Once" in the toolbar above the chart.
  2. In the lyric field that's off, put the cursor where you want to split.
  3. Tap "Next →" to send everything after the cursor to the start of the next measure.
  4. Tap "← Previous" to send everything before the cursor to the end of the previous measure.
  5. Tap "Done" when you're finished.
  • Sections without lyrics are folded up as "No lyrics (4 measures)" and similar. Tap to open one.
  • This screen opens at the measure you were looking at on the chart.

Push pickup lyrics to the right end of a measure

  1. To start a little to the right, put spaces before the words. Each space is about one character wide.
  2. To line words up with the right end of the measure, open "Edit All at Once" and put the cursor just before those words.
  3. Tap "Right from here". Everything after the cursor lines up with the right end of the measure.
  • One measure can hold both left-aligned and right-aligned lyrics. This is for a measure that ends one phrase and holds the pickup to the next.
  • To tidy several measures at once, select them and tap "Align Lyrics Left", "Align Lyrics Right" or "Remove Extra Spaces".
