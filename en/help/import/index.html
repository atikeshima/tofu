Bringing in printed music

Import a PDF

  1. In the song list, tap "Import" at the top right.
  2. Under "PDF Scores", tap "Import PDF from Files".
  3. Choose the PDF in the file browser. You can choose as many as you like.
  4. The "Pages" screen opens. Check the "Name" and the order of the pages.
  5. Tap "Done" at the top right.
  • If you choose two or more PDFs, the "Pages" screen is skipped and Tofu tells you how many were imported.
  • To stop, tap "Cancel" at the top left of the "Pages" screen, then "Stop Importing".
  • You can't enter chords into a PDF score, and it can't be transposed.
  • Tapping a PDF score in the list opens it directly in the performance screen.

Scan paper music with the camera

  1. In the song list, tap "Import", then "Scan with Camera". For photos you already have, tap "Choose from Photos".
  2. Scan the pages one after another. When you're done, tap "Save" on the iPad's scanner.
  3. When "Clean up the photos?" appears, tap "Clean Up" or "Keep As Is".
  4. If you used the camera, "Also save to Photos?" appears. Tap "Save" or "Don’t Save".
  5. Check the name and pages on the "Pages" screen, then tap "Done".
  • "Clean Up" makes the paper whiter and pencil markings darker. If you're not sure, choose "Keep As Is". You can clean up pages later.
  • While photos are being cleaned up, "Import Without Cleanup" imports them as they are, and "Cancel" stops the import completely.
  • If "Scan with Camera" isn't under "Import", this iPad can't scan documents.
  • The iPad's document scanner limits how many pages you can scan at once (it stopped at 24 when we tried). Add the rest with "Scan More Pages" on the "Pages" screen.
  • If you see "Tofu can’t use the camera. …", tap "Open Settings" and allow camera access for Tofu.
  • If you see "Couldn’t save to Photos. …", tap "Open Settings" and allow Photos access for Tofu. The score itself was imported.

Reorder pages or crop margins

  1. Tap the PDF score in the list. It opens on the performance screen.
  2. Tap the middle of the screen to show the performance menu (not the strips about 1 inch / 3 cm wide at the left and right edges, which turn pages), then tap "Edit Pages".
  3. To reorder, touch and hold the drag_handle under a page and drag it where you want it.
  4. To crop, tap crop ("Crop Margins") under the page.
  5. Drag the corners or edges of the frame to choose what to keep, then tap "Done".
  6. To rotate, straighten, clean up, rescan or delete a page, tap more_horiz under it.
  7. Tap "Done" at the top right when you're finished.
  • The more_horiz menu has "Move Earlier", "Move Later" (these move the page one place in the order), "Rotate Left", "Rotate Right", "Straighten", "Clean Up", "Retake" and "Delete Page".
  • "Apply to All Pages" crops every page with the same frame.
  • "Clean Up All Pages" cleans up every page at once.
  • To add pages, tap "Scan More Pages" or "Add from Photos".
  • None of this changes the original PDF. You can undo and redo each change.
  • "Cancel" at the top left throws away everything you changed since opening the screen.
  • Tap a page thumbnail to see it larger. Use chevron_left and chevron_right at the left and right edges to go to the previous or next page.

Turn a paper chord chart into an editable song (using an AI chatbot)

  1. In the song list, tap "Import", then "Import from AI".
  2. Tap "Copy Prompt". This copies the request text (the prompt) to give to the AI.
  3. In the AI app or website you use, paste the prompt and give it a photo of your chart.
  4. Copy the AI's whole reply, from start to finish. Copy it as is, without editing it.
  5. Come back to Tofu and tap "Paste the AI’s Reply".
  6. Under "What Will Be Imported", check the title, key and number of measures, then tap "Import" at the top right.
  7. The new song's chart opens. Compare the title and chords with your paper chart.
  • Tofu never talks to the AI itself. You pass the prompt and the reply back and forth.
  • If Tofu had to correct anything in the AI's reply, a list appears (for example "3 Fixes"). Check those spots first when you compare.
  • If you pasted the wrong thing, tap "Paste Again".
  • If the reply can't be used, Tofu shows the reasons. Your existing songs are unchanged.
  • "Copy All Reasons" copies them.

Rename or share a PDF score

PDF scores. For songs you made in Tofu, see "Change the title, key, time signature or tempo".

  1. Tap the PDF score in the list. It opens on the performance screen.
  2. Tap the middle of the screen to show the performance menu (not the strips about 1 inch / 3 cm wide at the left and right edges, which turn pages).
  3. To rename it, tap "Edit Pages", type the new "Name" and tap "Done".
  4. To send it to someone, tap "Share" and pick where to send it on the iPad's share screen.
  5. To leave the performance screen, tap "Close" at the right end of the performance menu.
  • If you haven't changed the PDF score, sharing sends the original PDF as it is. If you've cropped, written on or rearranged its pages, sharing sends a PDF with those changes on it.
  • In Split View, when Tofu's window is narrow, "Edit Pages" and "Share" are under more_horiz on the performance menu.
  • To duplicate or delete it, use more_horiz at the right end of its row in the song list.
  • You can give it the same name as another song or PDF score. Tofu won't add a number like "(2)".

Import a song from iReal Pro or another app (MusicXML)

MusicXML files exported by iReal Pro and other apps (file names ending in ".musicxml" or ".xml")

  1. In iReal Pro, open the song, share it and choose MusicXML. On the share screen, tap Tofu. from 1.6.0
  2. Tofu shows "Import MusicXML".
  3. Under "What Will Be Imported", check the title, key, number of measures and sections.
  4. Read "Before you import", then tap "Import" at the top right.
  5. The new song's chart opens. Compare it with the original.
  • For a MusicXML file saved in the Files app, tap "Import" in the song list, then "MusicXML (iReal Pro, etc.)".
  • It's added as a new song. Your existing songs are unchanged.
  • iReal Pro leaves empty measures and N.C. measures out of its MusicXML, so some measures may be missing. The tempo isn't imported.
  • The key is the one shown in iReal Pro when you exported (if you had transposed, the transposed key).
  • iReal Pro exports only sections that start with a double bar line. If sections are missing, add a double bar line before each section in iReal Pro and export again.
  • Chord types Tofu doesn't have come in as the root only. The screen lists them before you import.
  • Compressed MusicXML (file names ending in ".mxl") can't be imported.
  • To skip importing, tap "Cancel".
