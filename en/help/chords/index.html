Enter a single chord

Songs you made in Tofu that have at least one measure

  1. Tap the measure. The chord entry panel opens.
  2. Under "Beats in This Measure", tap the slot where the chord goes (for example "Beat 1").
  3. Under "Root", pick the note. For a C chord, tap "C".
  4. Under "Quality", pick the chord type. For a plain C major chord, tap "(none)", the one labeled "Major" underneath.
  5. Tap "Enter". The chord goes into the slot you picked.
  6. Tap "Done" at the top right to go back to the chart.
  • The panel stays open after "Enter".
  • It does not move to the next beat by itself. Tap the next slot first, then enter the next chord.
  • The type you picked last time may still be selected. Always check the type before tapping "Enter".
  • In a song with no chords yet, measure 1 shows a faint "Tap to enter a chord". from 1.4.0

Keep going into the next measure

  1. The top of the chord entry panel shows which measure you're in (for example "Measure 3").
  2. Tap the small arrow to the right of it (›) to go to the next measure, or the one on the left (‹) to go back.
  3. On the last measure, the right arrow becomes "Add a measure and go to it". Tap it to add one empty measure and move into it.
  4. Chords you have already used appear under "Chords in This Song (Most Used First)". Tap one to put it in the selected slot.
  5. Tap "Done" when you're finished.
  • A chord you have picked but not entered yet is entered when you move to another measure or tap "Done".
  • The panel stays on the same tab ("Chords", "Signs" or "Lyrics") when you move.

Enter chords like C7(♭9) and C/E

  1. Under "Root", pick the note. For C♯, tap "C" and then "♯".
  2. Above the chord types, turn on the groups you want to see: "Major Chords", "Minor Chords", "Dominant Chords" and "Other". You can turn on as many as you like.
  3. Tap a chord type. Each button shows the symbol in large type and its name below (for example "7" with "Dominant 7th").
  4. For the numbers in parentheses, such as ♭9, tap them under "Tensions (optional)". Tap again to remove one.
  5. For the note after the slash, like the E in C/E, tap "Add Bass Note" and pick the note under "Bass Note".
  6. Tap "Enter".
  • Example, C7(♭9): "C" → "7" → "♭9" → "Enter".
  • Example, C/E: "C" → "(none)" → "Add Bass Note" → "E" → "Enter".
  • To change the chord type after picking a bass note, tap "Back to Chord". The bass note stays.
  • For a spot with no chord (N.C.), tap "N.C." (labeled "No chord") and then "Enter".
  • The symbols on the type buttons follow the song's chord notation. In jazz style, Cm7 shows as "-7".
  • The groups you turn on stay on the next time.
  • If a symbol isn't on the buttons, you can type it as text in "Edit All at Once". Symbols Tofu doesn't know are kept as typed, and Tofu tells you.

Move a chord to another beat, or mark it as pushed

  1. Tap the measure and, under "Beats in This Measure", tap the slot with the chord.
  2. Tap "← Move Earlier" or "Move Later →". The chord moves to the next empty slot.
  3. For a chord that is played an eighth note early (a push), tap "Syncopation".
  4. A small eighth note with a tie appears at the top left of the chord. Tap again to remove it.
  5. To delete the chord, tap "Delete Chord". To clear every chord in the measure, tap "Clear Measure".
  • Moving skips over slots that already have a chord. Other chords stay where they are.
  • Chords can't be moved past the measure. To move a chord into another measure, retype it in "Edit All at Once".
  • "Syncopation" sits at the right end of the "Beats in This Measure" line. In a narrow window it says "Push".
  • "Syncopation" only works when the selected slot has a chord.
  • You can undo each of these with "Undo", the button with an arrow curving to the left.

Type chords for the whole song

  1. Tap "Edit All at Once" in the toolbar above the chart. The "Edit Lyrics and Chords" screen opens.
  2. In each measure's "Chords" field, type the chords separated by spaces (for example C . G .).
  3. A period (.) means the chord before it keeps going. The measure is split evenly by what you type, so four entries are a quarter note each.
  4. Press Return to go to the next field.
  5. Tap "Done" when you're finished.
  • You can type either style, such as Cm7 or C-7.
  • Repeat signs |: :| and the simile mark % go in the same field.
  • For a pushed chord, type ^ in front of it (for example ^F). "^ Syncopation" below the field does the same.
  • In A7(♭9,♭13), the comma inside the parentheses separates the tensions. Spaces inside parentheses don't start a new slot either. from 1.4.0
  • Tap a chord or mark in the row below to put it at the cursor.
  • Anything Tofu can't read becomes a period, and Tofu tells you. Symbols it doesn't know are kept as typed, and Tofu tells you that too.

Switch between jazz and standard chord symbols

  1. Tap the song title at the top of the chart screen to open "Song Settings".
  2. Under "Chord Notation", tap "Jazz" or "Standard".
  3. Tap "Done".
  • Jazz shows chords like C-7 and CΔ7. Standard shows Cm7 and Cmaj7.
  • You choose this for each song. The chords themselves don't change, only how they're written.
  • PDFs use the same style.
