Enter a single chord

Songs you made in Tofu that have at least one measure

  1. Tap the measure. The chord entry panel opens (at the bottom, or on the right in landscape).
  2. Under "Beats in This Measure", tap the beat button where the chord goes (for example "Beat 1").
  3. Under "Root", pick the note. For a C chord, tap "C".
  4. Under "Quality", pick the chord quality. For a plain C major chord, tap the "(none)" button (it says "Major" in small type underneath).
  5. Tap "Enter". The chord goes onto the beat you picked.
  6. Tap "Done" at the top right to go back to the chart.
  • The panel stays open after "Enter".
  • It does not move to the next beat by itself. Tap the next beat button first, then enter the next chord.
  • On an empty beat, "Quality" starts at "(none)". On a beat that already has a chord, that chord's quality is selected.
  • In a song with no chords yet, measure 1 shows a faint "Tap to enter a chord".
  • Tofu never drops a chord because a measure is crowded. If rows have a fixed number of measures, all the chords in that measure shrink to the same smaller size.
  • When only the bass note changes from the chord just before it in the same measure, Tofu shows just the slash and the bass note, like "C" then "/B". You still enter "C/B" as usual.

Keep going into the next measure

  1. The top of the chord entry panel shows which measure you're in (for example "Measure 3").
  2. Tap chevron_right to the right of it to go to the next measure, or chevron_left on the left to go back.
  3. On the last measure, chevron_right becomes "Add a measure and go to it". Tap it to add one empty measure and move into it.
  4. Chords you have already used appear under "Chords in This Song (Most Used First)". Tap one to put it on the selected beat.
  5. Tap "Done" when you're finished.
  • If you've picked a chord but haven't tapped "Enter" yet, Tofu enters it for you when you move to another measure or tap "Done".
  • The panel has "Chords", "Signs" and "Lyrics" tabs at the top. It stays on the same tab when you move.

Enter chords like C7(♭9) and C/E

  1. Under "Root", pick the note. For C♯, tap "C" and then "♯".
  2. Above the chord types, turn on the groups you want to see: "Major Chords", "Minor Chords", "Dominant Chords" and "Other". Only the types in the groups you turn on are shown. You can turn on as many as you like.
  3. Tap a chord type. Each button shows the symbol in large type and its name below (for example "m7" with "Minor 7th").
  4. For the numbers in parentheses, such as ♭9, tap them under "Tensions (optional)". Tap again to remove one.
  5. For the note after the slash in a slash chord, like the E in C/E, tap "Add Bass Note" and pick the note under "Bass Note".
  6. Tap "Enter".
  • Example, C7(♭9): "C" → "7" → "♭9" → "Enter".
  • Example, C/E: "C" → "(none)" → "Add Bass Note" → "E" → "Enter".
  • "Dominant Chords" holds the dominant 7th family, such as C7, C9 and C13.
  • After "Add Bass Note", the note buttons switch to picking the bass note. To change the chord type, tap "Back to Chord". The bass note stays.
  • For a spot with no chord (N.C.), tap "N.C." (labeled "No chord") and then "Enter".
  • The symbols on the type buttons follow the song's chord notation. In jazz style, Cm7 shows as "-7".
  • The groups you turn on stay on the next time.
  • If a symbol isn't on the buttons, you can type it as text in "Edit All at Once".

Move a chord to another beat, or mark it as pushed

  1. Tap the measure and, under "Beats in This Measure", tap the beat button with the chord.
  2. Tap "← Move Earlier" or "Move Later →". The chord moves to the next empty beat.
  3. For a chord played an eighth note early (a push, or anticipation), tap "Syncopation".
  4. A small tied eighth note appears at the top left of the chord on the chart. Tap again to remove it.
  5. To delete the chord, tap "Delete Chord". To clear every chord in the measure, tap "Clear Measure" (it appears when the measure has two or more chords).
  • Moving skips over beats that already have a chord. Other chords stay where they are.
  • Chords can't be moved past the measure. To move a chord into another measure, retype it in "Edit All at Once".
  • "Syncopation" sits at the right end of the "Beats in This Measure" row. In Split View, when Tofu's window is narrow, it says "Push".
  • "Syncopation" appears only when the selected beat has a chord.
  • You can undo each of these with undo ("Undo") at the top of the chord entry panel.

Type chords for the whole song

  1. Tap "Edit All at Once" in the toolbar above the chart. A screen titled "Edit Lyrics and Chords" opens.
  2. In each measure's "Chords" field, type the chords separated by spaces (for example C . G .).
  3. A period (.) means the chord before it keeps going. The measure is split evenly by the number of chords and periods together. In 4/4, four entries get one beat each, and two entries get two beats each.
  4. Press Return to go to the next field.
  5. When you're finished, tap "Done" above the keyboard. To go back to the chart, tap arrow_back_ios_new at the top left.
  • You can type either style, such as Cm7 or C-7.
  • Repeat signs |: :| and the measure repeat % go in the same field.
  • For a pushed chord, type ^ in front of it (for example ^F). The "^ Syncopation" button above the keyboard does the same.
  • In A7(♭9,♭13), the comma inside the parentheses separates the tensions. Spaces inside parentheses don't split chords either.
  • Tap a chord or mark in the row of buttons above the keyboard to put it at the cursor.
  • If Tofu can't read something as a chord, it turns it into a period (the chord before keeps going) and tells you which entries it changed.

Switch between jazz and standard chord symbols

  1. Tap the song title at the top of the chart screen to open "Song Settings".
  2. Under "Chord Notation", tap "Jazz" or "Standard".
  3. Tap "Done".
  • Jazz shows chords like C-7 and CΔ7. Standard shows Cm7 and Cmaj7.
  • You choose this for each song. The chords themselves don't change, only how they're written on screen and in PDFs.
