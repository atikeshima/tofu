Print or send a PDF

  1. On the chart screen, tap ios_share ("Share") at the top right.
  2. Tap "Share PDF".
  3. Under "Contents", choose "Chords + Lyrics", "Chords Only", "Lyric Sheet" or "UTAHON Style (Chords Over Lyrics)".
  4. If you want them, check "Include Title and Credits" and "Include Memo".
  5. If you want, change the "File Name". For example, when you send a version in another key, give it its own name. from 1.6.0
  6. Tap "Share".
  7. On the iPad's share screen, print or pick where to send it.
  • The "File Name" starts as the song title. For anything other than "Chords + Lyrics", Tofu adds the type to the name, like "Title (chords only)".
  • Some sheet music apps replace a PDF that has the same file name. When you send the song in different keys, give each one its own name.
  • Tofu doesn't remember a name you typed. Next time it starts from the song title again. ".pdf" is added when you share.
  • "Share" can't be tapped while the name is empty, contains / \ : * ? " < > |, or is too long. The reason appears below the field.
  • If signs don't match up, such as a D.S. with no segno, the "PDF Created" notice lists the measures with mismatched signs. The signs are printed as entered, so check them before you play.
  • If lyrics don't fit, they're printed slightly smaller.
  • Your last choice for "Include Title and Credits" is used next time.

Send a song to someone, or receive one

  1. To send, tap ios_share ("Share") at the top right of the chart screen and choose "Share File".
  2. Pick where to send it on the iPad's share screen.
  3. To receive, open the file you got in Mail or the Files app, and choose Tofu as the app to open it with.
  4. Or, in Tofu's song list, tap "Import", then "Import Song File", and choose the file.
  • Received songs go straight into your list.
  • If a song with the same name exists, a number like "(2)" is added to the title.
  • Files that can't be read aren't imported, and Tofu lists their names. Your existing songs are unchanged. To see why, choose the file again with "Import Song File".
  • The other person needs Tofu on their iPad too.
  • If you select two or more files in the Files app and share them, only the first one reaches Tofu. Share them one at a time.

Send a song to iReal Pro

Songs made in Tofu, on an iPad with iReal Pro installed

  1. On the chart screen, tap ios_share ("Share") at the top right, then "Send to iReal Pro". from 1.6.0
  2. If "Not sent to iReal Pro" or "Changes" appears, read through it.
  3. To send the lyrics of verse 1 too, check "Include lyrics (verse 1 only)".
  4. Tap "Send". iReal Pro opens and the song is added.
  • The song is sent in the key shown now. If you've transposed it, it arrives in the new key.
  • In iReal Pro, a measure has only four places for chords (in 4/4, the start of each beat). A chord placed between them, such as on the "and" of beat 2, is moved a little earlier.
  • Chords that are moved are listed under "Changes" before you send, like "Measure 3: G7 moves to an earlier beat".
  • If a measure has five or more chords, the fifth and later aren't sent. The rest of the song is.
  • Tempo, lyricist, release year, memo, verse 2 and later, handwriting, pushes (syncopation), colors, row breaks and a few other things aren't sent to iReal Pro.
  • The style arrives as "Medium Swing". You can change it in iReal Pro.
  • Long lyrics are cut short. In iReal Pro, lyrics may still overlap the next measure.
  • If iReal Pro isn't installed, you'll see "Couldn't open iReal Pro. …"
  • Songs are sent one at a time. A whole setlist can't be sent at once.

Back up everything

  1. In the song list, tap the Settings button (settings) at the top right.
  2. Under "Backup", tap "Export Everything".
  3. The iPad's share screen opens. Choose where to save the file (for example, somewhere in the Files app).
  4. When you see "Exported “Tofu …”" with the date and time, you're done.
  • The backup holds all your songs, PDF scores, setlists and settings, including handwriting and lyrics you've pasted but not yet placed.
  • The file is named like "Tofu 2026-10-16 19-05.tofu".
  • If you cancel on the share screen, nothing is saved.
  • To share one setlist with its songs and PDFs, use "Export with Songs and PDFs" under more_horiz on its row on the "Setlists" tab.

Move to another iPad or restore from a backup

  1. Make a backup file, as in "Back up everything".
  2. Send the file to the other iPad. AirDrop, Mail, iCloud Drive or any way you usually pass files will do.
  3. On the other iPad, open Tofu, go to Settings, and tap "Import Backup" under "Backup". Choose the file.
  4. The "Import “…”" screen shows how many songs, PDF scores and setlists the file has.
  5. If some of them are already on this iPad, choose what to do with them.
  6. To bring your settings across too, turn on "Also Restore Settings".
  7. Tap "Import". If you chose "Replace", Tofu asks again; tap "Replace".
  • "Add (Keep Both)": keeps both. Nothing on this iPad is removed.
  • "Replace (Overwrite the Ones on This iPad)": swaps the ones on this iPad for the ones in the file. This can't be undone.
  • "Skip (Keep the Ones on This iPad)": leaves the ones on this iPad as they are.
  • A song counts as "Already on this iPad" only if it's a copy of the same song. Songs that just share a title don't count.
  • "Also Restore Settings" is off by default, so a file from someone else won't change your own settings.
  • If some items can't be read, everything else is still imported, and the unreadable ones are listed with reasons.
  • When you replace your iPad, restoring the iPad from its own backup usually brings Tofu's contents along.

Make lyrics bigger

  1. In the song list, tap the Settings button (settings) at the top right.
  2. Under "Lyric Size", choose "Standard", "Large", "Extra Large" or "Largest".
  • This applies on screen and in PDFs, including the lyric sheet and UTAHON style.
  • Bigger lyrics can mean fewer measures per row.
  • It isn't saved in the song. It's how this iPad shows lyrics.

Use a handwritten chord font

  1. In the song list, tap the Settings button (settings) at the top right.
  2. Under "Chord Font", tap "Standard" or "Handwritten".
  • Handwritten looks like a jazz chart written by hand.
  • Along with the chords, signs such as segno, coda, D.S. and To Coda, ending numbers and time signatures are handwritten too. from 1.6.0
  • Lyrics, section names, the title and the memo stay in the standard font.
  • This applies on screen and in PDFs, including UTAHON style and the performance screen.
  • It isn't saved in the song. It's how this iPad shows chords.

Color the signs

  1. In the song list, tap the Settings button (settings) at the top right.
  2. Under "Sign Colors", pick a color for each group: "Repeats" (repeat signs ‖: :‖), "Segno", "Coda", "Fine" (Fine and D.C. al Fine), "Section Names" and "1st/2nd Endings".
  3. To remove a color, tap "None". To remove them all, tap "Clear All".
  • The color goes behind the sign, like a highlighter. It helps you spot the matching sign from a distance.
  • Signs in the same group get the same color (for example, the segno and D.S.).
  • The colors appear on screen and in PDFs.
  • They aren't saved in the song, so sending a song doesn't change anyone else's colors.
