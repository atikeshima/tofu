Sharing, backups and readability

Printing or sending a PDF, sending and receiving song files, backing up everything, moving to another iPad or restoring from a backup, making lyrics bigger, coloring signs

Print or send a PDF

  1. On the chart screen, tap "Share" at the top right. It's the square with an arrow pointing up out of it.
  2. Tap "Share PDF".
  3. Under "Contents", choose "Chords + Lyrics", "Chords Only", "Lyric Sheet" or "UTAHON Style (Chords Over Lyrics)".
  4. If you want them, check "Include Title and Credits" and "Include Memo".
  5. Tap "Share".
  6. On the iPad's share screen, print or pick where to send it.
  • If some signs don't match up (for example a D.S. with no segno), "PDF Created" appears with a list of the spots. The signs are printed as written, so check them before you play.
  • If lyrics don't fit, they're printed slightly smaller.
  • Your last choice for "Include Title and Credits" is used next time.

Send a song to someone, or receive one

  1. To send, tap the "Share" button on the chart screen and choose "Share File".
  2. Pick where to send it on the iPad's share screen.
  3. To receive, open the file you got in Mail or the Files app, and choose Tofu as the app to open it with.
  4. Or, in Tofu's song list, tap "Import", then "Import Song File", and choose the file.
  • Received songs go straight into your list.
  • If a song with the same name exists, a number is added.
  • Files that can't be read are refused with a reason. Your existing songs are unchanged.
  • The other person needs Tofu on their iPad too.

Back up everything

  1. In the song list, tap the gear button (Settings) at the top right.
  2. Under "Backup", tap "Export Everything".
  3. The iPad's share screen opens. Choose where to save the file (for example, somewhere in the Files app).
  4. When you see "Exported “Tofu …”" with the date and time, you're done.
  • The backup holds all your songs, PDF scores, setlists and settings, including handwriting and lyrics you haven't placed yet.
  • The file is named like "Tofu 2026-10-16 19-05.tofu".
  • If you cancel on the share screen, nothing is saved.
  • To pass on one setlist with its songs and PDFs, use "Export with Songs and PDFs" in the "…" menu of its row on the "Setlists" tab.

Move to another iPad or restore from a backup

  1. Make a backup file, as in "Back up everything".
  2. Send the file to the other iPad. Any way you usually pass files between iPads will do.
  3. On the other iPad, open Tofu, go to Settings, and tap "Import Backup" under "Backup". Choose the file.
  4. The "Import “…”" screen shows how many songs, PDF scores and setlists the file has.
  5. If some of them are already on this iPad, choose what to do with them.
  6. To bring your settings across too, turn on "Also Restore Settings".
  7. Tap "Import". If you chose "Replace", Tofu asks again; tap "Replace".
  • "Add (Keep Both)": keeps both. Nothing on this iPad is removed.
  • "Replace (Overwrite the Ones on This iPad)": swaps the ones on this iPad for the ones in the file. This can't be undone.
  • "Skip (Keep the Ones on This iPad)": leaves the ones on this iPad as they are.
  • "Already on this iPad" means the very same song, not just one with the same name.
  • "Also Restore Settings" starts off, so a file from someone else won't change your own settings.
  • If some items can't be read, everything else is still imported, and the unreadable ones are listed with reasons.
  • When you replace your iPad, restoring the iPad from its own backup usually brings Tofu's contents along.

Make lyrics bigger

  1. In the song list, tap the gear button (Settings) at the top right.
  2. Under "Lyric Size", choose "Standard", "Large", "Extra Large" or "Largest".
  • This applies on screen and in PDFs, including the lyric sheet and UTAHON style.
  • Bigger lyrics can mean fewer measures per row.
  • It isn't saved in the song. It's how this iPad shows lyrics.

Color the signs

  1. In the song list, tap the gear button (Settings) at the top right.
  2. Under "Sign Colors", pick a color for each group: "Repeats", "Segno", "Coda", "Fine", "Section Names" and "1st/2nd Endings".
  3. To remove a color, tap "None". To remove them all, tap "Clear All".
  • The color goes behind the sign, like a highlighter. It helps you spot the matching sign from a distance.
  • Signs in the same group get the same color (for example, the segno and D.S.).
  • The colors appear on screen and in PDFs.
  • They aren't saved in the song, so sending a song doesn't change anyone else's colors.
