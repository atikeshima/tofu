Getting ready with setlists

Make a setlist

  1. Tap the "Setlists" tab at the top of the song list.
  2. Tap "New Setlist", type a "Setlist Name" and tap "Create".
  3. When the screen is wide, such as in landscape, your songs and PDF scores are listed on the right. Tap one to add it to the end.
  4. When the screen is narrow, such as in portrait or split, tap "Add Songs", pick the songs, then tap "Add 3 Songs" (or however many you picked).
  5. To change the order, touch and hold the drag_handle at the left of a row and drag it. The arrow_upward arrow_downward buttons also work.
  6. Tap "Done".
  • You can add the same song twice. In the list on the right, songs already in the setlist show their position, like "✓ #1".
  • To take a song out, tap delete_outline ("Remove from Setlist"). There's no confirmation, but undo ("Undo") in the top bar brings it back.
  • To change the setlist later, open it and tap "Edit".
  • To rename, duplicate or delete a setlist, use more_horiz at the right end of its row on the "Setlists" tab.
  • When you have no setlists, "New Setlist" appears in the middle of the tab. If you have no songs or PDF scores yet, a "Go to Songs" button appears instead and takes you to the song list.
  • The list on the right and the "Add Songs" screen have a sort button (sort) too. It only orders the list you pick songs from. Tofu remembers this order for the next time you add songs. It doesn't change the order of the setlist or of your song list. from 1.6.0

Sort a setlist by name, date and more

  1. Open the setlist and tap "Edit". from 1.6.0
  2. Tap the sort button (sort) in the top bar.
  3. Choose "Name (A to Z)", "Date Created (Newest First)", "Date Modified (Newest First)" and so on.
  4. To go back to your own order, choose "Manual Order" in the same place.
  5. Tap "Done".
  • Sorting never erases your manual order. Choose "Manual Order" to get it back anytime.
  • While sorted, a line such as "Sorted by Name (A to Z)" appears above the list. You play the songs in that order too.
  • If you move a row or add a song while sorted, Tofu asks "Make the order shown now your manual order? …" It doesn't ask when the sorted order is the same as your manual order, since nothing would be lost.
  • In that question, "Use This Order" makes the order you see your new manual order, and your previous manual order is lost. "Cancel" changes nothing.
  • undo ("Undo") also puts back a change of sort order.

Change a song's key for one setlist

  1. Open the setlist and tap "Edit".
  2. Tap the key button on the song's row (for example "Song’s Key (G)").
  3. Choose "Song’s Key (G)" or one of the 12 keys. Once you choose, the button reads like "Key A (+2)". (+2) means two half steps up from the song's key.
  4. Tap "Done".
  • The song itself isn't changed. The key is kept only in this setlist.
  • You can also change it while playing, with "Key" in the performance menu.
  • PDF scores have no key on their rows.

Play a setlist, or switch songs mid-set

  1. Open the setlist and tap "Perform". It starts from the first song.
  2. Turning past the last page of a song takes you straight to the next song.
  3. To jump to another song, tap the song title at the bottom of the screen and choose a song from the list.
  • To start from a later song, tap that song's row instead of "Perform". It opens at the song's first page.
  • "Songs" in the performance menu opens the same list.
  • The bottom of the screen shows the song number, title, key and page.
  • A hint about the song title at the bottom appears the first time you play a setlist (or the next time, if the page-turning hint appears that time).
