Getting ready with setlists

Making a setlist, adding, reordering and removing songs, changing a song's key for one setlist, playing through a setlist, switching songs in the middle of a set

  • Make a setlist
  • Change a song's key for one setlist
  • Play a setlist, or switch songs mid-set

Make a setlist

  1. Tap the "Setlists" tab at the top of the song list.
  2. Tap "New Setlist", type a "Setlist Name" and tap "Create".
  3. On a wide screen, your songs and PDF scores are listed on the right. Tap one to add it to the end.
  4. On a narrow screen, tap "Add Songs", pick the songs, then tap "Add 3 Songs" (or however many you picked).
  5. To change the order, touch and hold the "≡" handle at the left of a row and drag it. The up and down arrow buttons also work.
  6. Tap "Done".
  • You can add the same song twice. Songs already in the setlist show their position, like "✓ #1".
  • To take a song out, tap the trash can ("Remove from Setlist"). There's no confirmation, but "Undo" brings it back.
  • To change the setlist later, open it and tap "Edit".
  • To rename, duplicate or delete a setlist, use the "…" button at the right end of its row on the "Setlists" tab.
  • When you have no setlists, "New Setlist" appears in the middle of the tab.

Change a song's key for one setlist

  1. Open the setlist and tap "Edit".
  2. Tap the key on the song's row (for example "Key G").
  3. Choose "Song’s Key" or one of the 12 keys.
  4. Tap "Done".
  • The song itself isn't changed. The key is kept only in this setlist.
  • You can also change it while playing, with "Key" in the performance menu.
  • PDF scores have no key on their rows.

Play a setlist, or switch songs mid-set

  1. Open the setlist and tap "Perform". It starts from the first song.
  2. To start from a later song, tap that song's row. It opens at the song's first page.
  3. Pages turn straight through from one song to the next.
  4. To jump to another song, tap the song title strip at the bottom of the screen and choose a song from the list.
  • "Songs" in the performance menu opens the same list.
  • The strip at the bottom shows the song number, title, key and page.
  • The first time you play a setlist, a callout points to the title strip.
